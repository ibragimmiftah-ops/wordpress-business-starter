Business settings, contact shortcodes and lightweight lead capture for the Business Starter theme.

== Installation ==
1. Upload to wp-content/plugins/business-starter-core.
2. Activate in Plugins.
3. Open Business in wp-admin and fill company details.
4. Add [business_contact_form] to a page.
5. Configure real SMTP/API email delivery before production handoff.
